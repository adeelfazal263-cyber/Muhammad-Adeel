The 3-Step Gentle Layering Ritual

01. Cool & Prep

Splash & Silk Balm

Begin right after a warm shower with a chilled botanical Splash and a swipe of Cotton Peony Deodorant to create a clean, hydrated canvas.

02. Anchor Pulse Points

Roll-On & Eau de Parfum

Glide a nourishing Roll-On across wrists and collarbone, then mist one or two sprays of Perfume above to lock in soft peony and vanilla warmth for 8+ hours.

03. Veil & Refresh

Hair & Linen Mist

Finish with a cloud of Rosewater Mist or Dewy Jasmine Body Spray over scarves and hair lengths for a gentle trail that refreshes whenever you move.
